Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Home assistant is a private, single-user application operated by Andrew Pang for his own home automation. It is not a public or commercial service and no one else is invited to use it.

Data accessed

Through Google's APIs, the application accesses only the data needed to operate the owner's own Home Assistant installation with his own Google account (for example, account identity and any Google service data the owner explicitly connects to his Home Assistant).

How data is used

Data is used solely to provide home-automation functions on the owner's own systems. Data is processed on the owner's own Home Assistant server in his home.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared with, or disclosed to any third party. Data is not used for advertising, analytics, or training of machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

Any data stored by the application resides on the owner's own hardware. Access tokens are held only by the owner's Home Assistant installation and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account permissions page.
